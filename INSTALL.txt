LINGUA update: redesign, level-aware training, reliable saving, more German content

1. In your repo, DELETE the old contents of docs/ (keep docs/ADDING_CONTENT.md).
2. Unzip this over the repo root, allowing overwrite. It contains src/, docs/, index.html and vite.config.ts.
3. Commit and push EVERYTHING, including every file in docs/assets/.
4. Open the app once, then close and reopen it. A banner "A new version is ready" appears when future updates ship; tap Update.

What changed
- Placement now sets your training level. B2 means B2 words, grammar, reading and listening first. You can also pick a level yourself.
- Progress saves with retry, a second copy in local storage, and a save on tab close. The service worker no longer reloads the page by itself.
- Onboarding and placement-test drafts survive a refresh.
- New design: level badges and ladder, Home level card, cleaner language switcher, quieter Teacher.
- More German vocabulary at every level.
